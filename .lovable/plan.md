# 72-Hour Subscriber Bonus Banner

## What will change
- Add a highly visible campaign banner near the top of the landing page.
- Show a live 72-hour countdown and progress bar during the campaign window.
- When the countdown ends, remove the timer and countdown bar while keeping a bold green message that the 300% new-subscriber bonus is ongoing.

## Technical details
- Use one fixed, site-wide campaign deadline so visitors see the same countdown rather than receiving a fresh 72 hours on each visit.
- Keep the countdown accessible and update it once per second; use the existing design tokens and landing-page styles.
- Confirm the landing page metadata remains accurate and verify the countdown and expired states in the preview.
